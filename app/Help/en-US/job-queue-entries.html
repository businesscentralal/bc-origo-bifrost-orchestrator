Job Queue Entries

Bifrost Nornir extends the standard Job Queue Entries list so that an existing job queue entry can be placed under the orchestrator's supervision without being redefined from scratch.

Added field

Field Description
Orchestrator Enabled Shows whether the orchestrator is monitoring this job queue entry. When it is, the orchestrator recreates and restarts the entry according to the retry policy on the matching orchestrator entry.

Added action

Action Description
Add to Job Queue Orchestrator Creates an orchestrator entry from the selected job queue entry, so the orchestrator monitors it and restarts it when it fails. The schedule of the existing entry is carried over.

Tips

  • Once an entry is under orchestrator control, maintain the schedule on the Job Queue Orchestrator Entry Card rather than here.
  • All orchestrator entries are also listed on Job Queue Orchestrator Setup.
